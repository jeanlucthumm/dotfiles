Archive a decision into `~/memory/decisions.md`, the single cross-project decisions archive.

First read the header of `~/memory/decisions.md` — it states the admission bar (interview storytelling, ~10 minutes of discussion with a hiring manager a year out) and the required entry shape. Most decisions do NOT qualify; those go in the project work log or PR description instead. Never create per-project decisions files.

- Only archive decisions that are actually made. Live/open decision points stay in the project's evolving doc in `~/memory/projects/`.
- Ask: is this a *story* (tension, stakes, a bet, a second act), or just a correct call? Only stories get in.
- Prefer consolidating related decisions into one narrative over adding new entries.
- Structure: one `#` section per project, entries newest-last within a section.
- Capture: what made it hard (tension/constraints), the options considered and why the rejected ones lost, the call made, and its impact/outcome. Implementation mechanics get at most one line each.
- After archiving, delete the decision-point content from the project doc, leaving a one-line work-log residue pointing at the archive.
